Meet Your AI Companion

Dr. Heinz Doofenshmirtz has officially retired from his evil schemes and discovered something much more rewarding - helping people feel better about themselves!

This project combines the power of artificial intelligence with empathetic conversation to create a safe space for mental health support that's available anytime, anywhere. Built using a React.js front-end for a dynamic user experience and a Python Flask API to handle our advanced sentiment analysis models, this is more than just a chatbot; it's a companion.

Built by a Team of Students

This project was created by a passionate team of college students who believe that mental health support should be accessible, engaging, and available to everyone.
